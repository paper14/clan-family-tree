# Button

The one button: quiet by default, **primary** (lineage) at most once per view for the thing the page is for — "Save person", "Save children". **danger** only for removing data or a parent link; **link** for inline navigation ("Open in tree").

- Consumer provides: the label (children), `onClick`, `type="submit"` inside forms.
- Labels are verb first, sentence case: "Add another child", never "SUBMIT" or "OK".
- Don't put two primary buttons side by side; the second becomes quiet.
