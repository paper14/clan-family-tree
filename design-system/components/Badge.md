# Badge

One or two words of status, always a word — never colour alone.

- Tones: `living` (Living), `warn` (Unplaced — no parent linked yet), `gilt` (Founder), `lineage` (Clan line), neutral (Adopted, Step, Foster, Link hidden), `danger` (Cycle, only beside the error).
- Consumer provides: the word as children. Capitals come from CSS; write it in sentence case.
